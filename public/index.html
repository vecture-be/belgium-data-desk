What the labels mean

Official source
The reference publisher for this kind of data, such as Statbel for population.
Open licence
The publisher allows reuse, including commercial reuse.
Own terms
The publisher uses its own licence text. Read it before you reuse the data.
No licence stated
The publisher did not state a licence. Ask them before you reuse the data.
Download works
We downloaded the file on the date shown and it was in the stated format. We check every week.
