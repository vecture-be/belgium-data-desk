About

Find. Understand. Trust.

Belgian open data is spread over federal, regional and city portals. Belgium Data Desk puts it in one search and lets you check a dataset before you rely on it.

Find

One search over data.gov.be, the regional and city portals and the main national registers, in Dutch, French or English. For common needs, such as population or the enterprise register, the official source comes first.

The same search works inside ChatGPT and Claude, and the Belgian Open Data social accounts share datasets worth knowing.

Understand

Open a dataset to see its columns, sample rows and a profile of the file: what one row stands for, the period it covers and which codes it can be joined on.

Query it with SQL in your browser and test whether two datasets really join. Nothing is uploaded.

Trust

Every week we download the files and check that the link works, the format is what the catalogue says, the data is as recent as the publisher promises and which columns identify a row.

These checks show what we found. They do not prove the figures are right.

The publishers remain the source

We link to the original for every dataset, and you can report a problem to its publisher from the dataset page. Questions or ideas: info@vecture.be.
